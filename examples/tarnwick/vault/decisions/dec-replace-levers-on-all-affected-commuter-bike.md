# Replace levers on all affected commuter bikes

## Decision
Every commuter bike built between October and December 2025 gets new levers, fitted by the dealer. Part of [[prj-brake-lever-recall|Brake lever recall 2026]].

## Why
A recall of the whole batch is simpler than testing each lever.
