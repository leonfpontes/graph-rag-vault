# Calvane takes over C27 tube joining from January

## Decision
From January 2027 the tubes of the C27 are joined by Calvane Tubeworks, our contract partner
in Porto. Tarnwick keeps heat treatment, paint and final assembly.

## Why
The jig line has no room until March, and the partner's unit cost is within 3% of ours.
