# Switch Ridgeline battery cells to Voltaro

## Decision
From January 2027 the Ridgeline packs use Voltaro cells. Ampcore keeps the cargo bike packs
until its contract ends.

## Why
Ampcore's twelve-week delay would have moved the [[prj-ridgeline-emtb|Ridgeline]] launch, and
the Voltaro cells passed every lab test.
