# Commuter 2027 frame in 6061 aluminium

## Decision
The [[prj-commuter-2027|C27]] frame is made of 6061 aluminium, heat-treated after joining.

## Why
Lighter than our steel frames by 600 g and cheaper to paint. Steel stays on the Gravel GX.
