# Rear rack v2 rated for 27 kg

## Decision
The new rack is rated for 27 kg and takes a child seat. Part of [[prj-rear-rack-v2|Rear rack v2]].

## Why
Dealers lose sales to cargo bikes because of the old 20 kg limit.
