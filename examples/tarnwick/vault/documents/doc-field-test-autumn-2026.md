# Field test notes, autumn 2026

## Riders
Twelve outside riders, three weeks on the [[prj-ridgeline-emtb|e-MTB prototype]].

## Complaints
A creak from the rear shock bushing after wet rides, and a display that is hard to read in
low sun. Range was praised by everyone.
