# Ridgeline e-MTB

## Summary
A full-suspension electric mountain bike with 150 mm of travel and a mid-drive motor, for a
spring 2027 launch. The first Tarnwick bike built for trails rather than roads.

## Status
The second prototype round ended in September. Battery integration is the open risk; the cell
choice is recorded in [[dec-switch-battery-voltaro|the October decision]] and the pack in
[[doc-battery-spec-v3|the battery spec]].
