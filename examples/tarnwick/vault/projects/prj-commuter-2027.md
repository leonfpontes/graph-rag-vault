# Commuter 2027 frame

## Summary
A new frame for the commuter range from the 2027 model year: lower step-over, integrated rear
rack mounts and room for 45 mm tyres.

## Plan
Prototype frames in August, series production from January 2027. Frames will be welded in our
own frame shop in Tarnwick, as all commuter frames have been since 2015.
